# The World Today — 22 November 2026 in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 22 November's world news in one image".

A newspaper front-page style summary of Sunday 22 November 2026:
COP31 climate talks collapse with no deal, Russian missiles hit Kyiv homes,
Iran strikes Bahrain as tankers are hit in Hormuz, Mexico captures cartel
kingpin 'El Chapo Isidro', and US Democrats win the House with 258 seats.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024x1024) to `/output/entry.png`.
No network access; output is byte-identical on every run.
